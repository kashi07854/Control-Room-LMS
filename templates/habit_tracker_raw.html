🚀 The 5 Rules of Billionaire Building

1. Solve a $1B problem. Small problems = small money. Find something millions of people desperately need.

2. Scale infinitely. Software, AI, digital products — these have near-zero marginal cost. One code = infinite users.

3. Own equity, not salary. No one became a billionaire on salary. Start or join a startup with equity.

4. Compound for 10+ years. Every billionaire spent a decade+ obsessively focused. No shortcuts.

5. Reinvest everything. Take profit, put it back in. Scale until the market caps out.
